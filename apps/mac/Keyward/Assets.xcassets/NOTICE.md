# Third-party marks

## Brand icons

`*Logo.imageset` files come from two places:

- **[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org)** — the SVG files are CC0-1.0. Brand
  colours were applied by us; the upstream files ship uncoloured.
- **Beacon** (`beacon-mac`, same author) — the LLM-provider marks.

**The icon files are freely licensed. The trademarks are not.** They are included
solely to identify the service a stored secret belongs to, which is nominative
use, and they are never used in Keyward's own branding, marketing, icon or
website. Several brands (notably Stripe and AWS) publish trademark guidelines
worth reading before any use beyond this.

**Slack, Twilio and Groq are deliberately absent.** Simple Icons removed the first
two after trademark requests from the brands themselves. A brand that has asked
not to be redistributed should not be redistributed; those services fall back to
the monogram like any other unknown one, which costs nothing.

If a brand asks us to remove a mark, remove it — the monogram fallback means
nothing breaks.
